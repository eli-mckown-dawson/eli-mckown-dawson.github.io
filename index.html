I write about elections and polling, and build the averages, forecasts and ratings behind the stories.

I'm Senior Elections Analyst at Silver Bulletin in Washington, DC. I run the presidential approval average with Nate Silver, maintain our polling database and pollster ratings, and design the charts that go with them.

Before that I was a contributor at YouGov and a research fellow at the LeRoy Collins Institute. I hold an MSc in Statistics from the London School of Economics and a BS in Political Science from Florida State University.
